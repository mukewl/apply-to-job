# Applicant profile

Everything personal lives here. The skill reads this file and never hardcodes your
details, so sharing the skill means sharing everything except this file and
`config.json`.

Fill it in honestly. The skill's whole value is that it refuses to invent things, and
it can only do that if it knows what is true.

## Work authorisation

The single most expensive thing to get wrong. Be specific about **countries**, not
regions — a national student or work permit does not travel across the EU.

- Can work without sponsorship in: `Germany, India`
- Needs sponsorship in: `everywhere else, including the rest of the EU`
- Permit expires / changes on: `2026-12-31`
- Post-study route available: `yes - 18-month job-seeker visa after graduation`

## Languages

The skill will not claim a level above what you put here, ever.

- English: never claim above `Fluent`
- German: never claim above `B1`
- French: never claim above `A1`

Working rule: `apply only to roles that can be done in English`

## CV masters

One per market. The address and phone number differ, and sending the wrong one is a
silent failure — the document looks right and the phone is unreachable.

| Market | File |
|---|---|
| Europe | `CV/master_cv_europe.tex` |
| India  | `CV/master_cv_india.tex` |

- Default headline (the line under the name in the master): `Growth Marketing Manager`
  The skill replaces this with the posting's own job title on every application.

## Protected numbers

The achievements that must survive tailoring. The skill keeps at least two of them in
the summary of every CV, so the document never becomes generic.

Protected numbers:
- `32% cost-per-acquisition reduction`
- `4x return on ad spend`
- `50,000+ signups`
- `12M+ organic views`

Keep at least: `2`

## Trimming

When a tailored CV runs long, cut in this order. The entry named under "Trim first" is
the only experience block a script may remove on its own.

- Trim first: `Junior Analyst`
- Never drop: `Head of Growth, Co-founder`

## Known gaps — say these plainly, never paper over them

List what you genuinely cannot do. The skill names these in its report and in outreach
where relevant, because being caught overstating is worse than being under-qualified.

- `SEO - paid media background, not organic`
- `No traditional media planning (TV, BARC, GRP, print, radio)`
- `No formal consumer research or panel work`
- `Team leadership is thin - led five people once`
- `B2B enterprise product marketing is a stretch`

## Targets

- Locations: `Germany, Remote-EU`
- Salary expectation: `EUR 60-75k`

## Hard no-go rules
